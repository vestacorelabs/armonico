# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 grand recorded note by note.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source of the original recordings: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
These 30 files (every third semitone from A0 to C8, one velocity layer, MP3) are the
subset prepared by the Tone.js project: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

Used unchanged, as the "Grand piano" sound of the lesson screen.
