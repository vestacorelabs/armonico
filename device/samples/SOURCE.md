# Piano samples of the device

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 grand recorded note by note.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source of the original recordings: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
These 30 files are taken from the lossless FLAC edition of the same recordings
(48 kHz, 24 bit, stereo): https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

The notes are the same 30 as the lesson screen's MP3s (every third semitone from A0 to C8)
and the same recording of each: velocity layer 8 of 16, the layer the Tone.js subset was
made from.

Changes: cut to the first 7.5 seconds, mixed down to mono and stored as 16 bit FLAC at
48 kHz, the format the device plays. Nothing else in the sound was changed.

The device renders its key sounds from these files ("armonico_pi.py prepare").
