# Installer music: sources

The installer can play these pieces on the keyboard while it works. Each file is a short excerpt of an edition from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), all released into the public domain. The excerpts were cut at a musical cadence, set to a single piano voice, and some were brought closer to a usual performance tempo.

| File | Piece | Composer | Excerpt | Mutopia |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ode_to_joy.mid | Ode to Joy (hymn setting) | L. van Beethoven | complete | [528](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528) |
| minuet_in_g.mid | Minuet in G, BWV Anh. 114 | C. Petzold (attributed to J. S. Bach) | complete | [75](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=75) |
| fur_elise.mid | Für Elise, WoO 59 | L. van Beethoven | opening theme, middle phrase and return | [931](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=931) |
| rondo_alla_turca.mid | Piano Sonata K. 331, 3rd movement | W. A. Mozart | first section and the A major refrain, at 1.85 times the edition's tempo | [108](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=108) |
| eine_kleine_nachtmusik.mid | Serenade K. 525, 1st movement | W. A. Mozart | first 17 bars, the string quartet played on piano | [900](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=900) |
| prelude_in_c.mid | Prelude in C, BWV 846 | J. S. Bach | first 19 bars, at 1.4 times the edition's tempo | [5](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=5) |
