# Installer music: sources

The installer can play these pieces on the keyboard while it works, and they are the first songs to learn. Each file is the MIDI file of an edition from the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), all released into the public domain, with the hands (or the instruments) on tracks of their own as the edition has them. Two are the whole file as it is. Three are its opening, cut where the music comes to rest: every note, length and loudness is the edition's.

| File | Piece | Composer | What is in the file | Mutopia |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ode_to_joy.mid | Ode to Joy (hymn setting) | L. van Beethoven | the whole file, unchanged | [528](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528) |
| minuet_in_g.mid | Minuet in G, BWV Anh. 114 | C. Petzold (attributed to J. S. Bach) | the whole file, unchanged | [75](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=75) |
| fur_elise.mid | Für Elise, WoO 59 | L. van Beethoven | the first 22 bars: the theme, its middle phrase and the return | [931](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=931) |
| rondo_alla_turca.mid | Piano Sonata K. 331, 3rd movement | W. A. Mozart | the first section and the A major refrain, at 1.85 times the edition's tempo (the edition plays at 60 to the quarter note) | [108](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=108) |
| eine_kleine_nachtmusik.mid | Serenade K. 525, 1st movement | W. A. Mozart | the first 17 bars, the four string parts | [900](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=900) |

Two short traditional tunes were written for Armonico from the melodies themselves, one note at a time, as songs for the beginner level. The tunes are traditional and in the public domain, and the files are part of this project.
